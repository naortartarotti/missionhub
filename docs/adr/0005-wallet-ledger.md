# ADR 0005 — Wallet: internal double-entry ledger, funds held by the payment provider
**Decision:** Organizations get a wallet (one balance per currency, `pending` and `available`). Balances live in an internal double-entry ledger in Postgres (`wallet_accounts`, `wallet_transactions`, `wallet_entries`); the money itself is held by a licensed payment provider (Stripe Connect planned), never in a platform bank account. All writes go through one SECURITY DEFINER engine, `app.wallet_post`. This supersedes the "no funds move through the platform" rule of the original finance design; the evidence-based commission ledger stays, and remains the fallback for payments made outside the platform.
**Why:** Job owners need to pay the sourcing recruiter and the platform in one step, and recruiters need a balance they can cash out or send to partners. A ledger we own gives atomic transfers, an audit trail and reconciliation against the provider; delegating custody keeps the platform out of holding client money directly.
**Consequences:** Wallets belong to organizations, not users (placements are org-to-org). No currency conversion. Corrections are compensating transactions. Recruiter-to-recruiter transfers ship disabled (`wallet_settings.transfers_enabled`) and are restricted to partner organizations. **Open, needs legal review before production:** stored value plus user-directed transfers may be regulated money transmission (MSB/FINTRAC in Canada); provider terms on how long funds may be held.
**Provider integration (added):** Stripe via REST, no SDK (`src/server/integrations/stripe.ts`): Checkout for money in, Connect Express accounts + transfers for money out ("separate charges and transfers": funds stay in the platform balance until withdrawn). Everything is inert until `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` are set. See `docs/payments.md`.
