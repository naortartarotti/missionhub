# Product flows

**Candidate owner (A):** Candidates → add/import (drafts) → record processing authorization → *Activate for matching* → Opportunities (Strong / Partial / Not eligible) → *Why this match* drawer → *Review match* → choose fields & documents, record job-specific authorization, confirm interest → Submit → Referral detail (timeline, messages) → confirm collaboration agreement when B accepts → follow progress → Commissions (projected → payable → paid after confirming payout receipt).

**Job owner (B):** Jobs → new (paste description → suggested requirements → review) → open to partner referrals (permanent jobs only, terms card required, see N2 below) & choose pre-referral visibility → job page shows anonymized match counts → Referrals › Received → review snapshot → Accept / Decline (reason, or a prior-presentation objection, see N1 below) / Request info → Interviewing → Offer → Report hire (from the frozen terms, see N2) → Commissions: record invoice → record client payment → (after platform verification) record payout & platform remittance.

**Job terms card (N2):**
- *Hiring side (B):* the job form's terms card sets the fee basis (% of base, % of total cash, or flat), the rate or amount, an optional minimum, search type, guarantee and remedy, client payment terms and the ownership window (6–12 months). The split is shown read-only. Opening to partners needs a complete card, a permanent contract type and the direct-client attestation, ticked again on every save that changes terms. Every terms change creates a new numbered version; saves that change nothing else don't. Referral-open jobs without a card, contract jobs, and % cards whose currency no longer matches the salary are hidden from partners, and the owner sees a nudge with the reason. On a new version, partners with live referrals are notified in-app that their referrals keep the terms they were sent under. Report hire: guarantee and currency are read-only. B enters the actual base (and target bonus on total cash; nothing on flat), the computed fee is shown, and a different invoiced fee needs a reason, which notifies the submitter.
- *Sourcing side (A):* the opportunity and submit screen show the current terms, the network-rule lines (draft until N7) and, when the job shares its salary, the estimated fee and share. Submitting freezes the version A saw, the commission rule and the estimate. If B changed the terms meanwhile, A sees **terms changed** and submits again under the new version. The referral page and agreement dialog show the frozen terms. The ownership date is set at delivery from the job's window; referrals sent before N2 show "Terms not recorded" and keep 12 months.
- *Ledger:* A's share is never computed on less than the frozen terms and is paid as the client pays. When B discounts without an amendment, B funds A's share of the discount. The platform share is on the fee actually collected (PD-049).
- *Platform admin:* /admin/referrals → look up a referral → record a concierge amendment before the hire, when both orgs asked in writing (reason and both references, admin-only). Both orgs are notified. /admin shows partner-visible jobs and referral-open jobs hidden for missing terms.

**Duplicate and prior-presentation protection (N1):**
- *Sourcing side (A):* Submit → the server checks the candidate's identifiers (at least one of email, phone or LinkedIn is required) against earlier referrals to the same job, or to the same hiring org's jobs for the same client. Possible results: created; **already presented** (stopped, one neutral message, support reference); **may already be in process** (pending warning → acknowledge → submit again). Candidate-confirmed path: if someone else's referral is delivered first, this referral closes as already presented and the candidate sees "already sent". Withdraw keeps first position unless A ticks **Release** (two steps, irreversible). A delivered referral that was withdrawn or cancelled and still counts can be released later. If B files an objection, A sees the panel (dates, evidence type, SHA-256, note, contest deadline in ET with business days left) → **Contest** before the deadline → referral dispute.
- *Hiring side (B):* Referrals › Received → Decline → **File objection** (prior presentation) within 5 business days of delivery, while the referral is in review: a file (PDF/PNG/JPEG, scanned) or an earlier MissionHub referral, the prior-process date, an attestation and an optional note. No objection in bulk decline; one per referral; accepting ends the option.
- *Platform admin:* /admin › Disputes → referral disputes next to placement disputes → Uphold (decline stands; the referral stops counting) or Reject (back to review with a fresh clock). An uncontested objection becomes final at the contest deadline. /admin shows duplicate-protection metrics and abuse flags.
- Notifications are in-app only until the N7 email pipeline ships. Business days are Monday to Friday in ET, minus the Ontario and Québec holiday table.

**Referral-level claims and two-sided hire reporting (N3):**
- *Jobs:* a job opens to partners only with the client's real name (it stays internal when "Hide client" is on). Existing open jobs without one are hidden from partners, with the "add the client's name" nudge.
- *Hiring side (B), recording a hire:* **Record hire** on any referral that still counts, not only `offer`: in progress, or declined / withdrawn / rejected / cancelled / SLA-released within window end + 9 months. Fields: the acceptance date (or "unknown"), the start date, this job or another role at the same client, then the N2 fee fields.
  - On a referral with a confirmed agreement, the record creates a placement.
  - On a referral without one, it creates a deemed agreement from the frozen terms (behind a flag, off in production until counsel), or a hire recorded without a split.
  - On a released referral, the hire is recorded without a split.
  - Not while B's own objection is open. A late record is flagged to admins only.
- *Sourcing side (A), reporting a hire:* **Report a hire** on a delivered, counting referral, until window end + 6 months (+ 9 with the "process started inside the window" statement). Content: the employer as known, approximate start month, role, how A learned, 1–5 dated evidence items (links visible only to A and admins), optional scanned files (admins only beyond A), a statement and an attestation. Every refusal reads the same. While a report is pending, A can't release the candidate for that client.
- *Hiring side (B), answering:* the report panel offers **Confirm and record hire** (records the hire and closes the report as confirmed) or **Contest** within 10 business days, with one of four reasons and evidence; contesting opens a hire-report dispute. A may add one rebuttal within 5 business days. If B records the hire any other way, a pending report closes as "confirmed by the hiring recruiter". Reminders go out at business days 5 and 8. With no answer by the deadline, the report goes to the admin queue: it's confirmed automatically only when N7 records delivered emails to the job owner and another admin of B.
- *Fee-pending placements:* an upheld (or deemed) report creates a placement without fee details. B enters them within 10 business days (no invoice or payment before that); overdue fee details create an admin task (Rule 6.4 notice) and notify B.
- *Release:* "Release later" also works on declined and rejected referrals. Both sides see "Protected while in progress and until {date} ET"; B's line adds "unless the sourcing recruiter released the candidate".
- *Authorization check (RD-029):* B can ask for one inside a contested dispute. An admin opens it; if there's a recent-change note or the challenger brought evidence, A has 5 business days to send independent evidence. Invalid → the presentation is void: it stops counting, no fee, the consent is disputed, and any pending report closes.
- *Platform admin:* /admin/hire-reports covers the queue (to decide, waiting, tasks, recently closed) and the detail (both client keys, every link and file, delivery records, signals, admin fields), plus **Uphold** (fee-pending placement, or recorded without a split) or **Reject** with a finding. "Not this client" shows A only that. No amounts. HA-6 flags orgs with 2 rejected reports in 90 days; HA-1, HA-9 and the metrics are Ops SQL (`docs/ops/n3-hire-signals.sql`).

**Org admin:** Settings › Team (invite by one-time link, roles, suspend), Privacy requests, Audit log.

**Platform admin (/admin):** metrics, evidence verification queue, disputes, referral terms amendments (N2), commission rule versions, org suspension.

Every screen has empty, loading, error and success states. Statuses always pair an icon with text.
