## Member-facing text

### Introduction

These rules are how members of the Workatent network work together. They protect your candidates, your clients and your fees, and they are part of the agreement your organization accepts to join.

Workatent is software and a record of what happened. We match candidates and jobs, keep dated records, and decide what happens on Workatent when rules are broken. We are not a recruiter, we don't hold or move money between members, and we don't decide money disputes.

> *Internal:* RD-010 (software and record), PD-053.2 (platform effects only). ⚖️ positioning wording must match the Terms of Service.

### 1. Membership and acceptance

**1.1** Your organization is the member. An organization admin accepts these rules for the organization, and every member accepts them before using the network. ⚖️
> *Internal:* TE-01, TE-02, RD-002. Versioned acceptance per org and per member is P0-2.

**1.2** Your organization is responsible for what its members do on Workatent. A breach by a member is a breach by the organization. ⚖️
> *Internal:* TE-02 (adapted: org liability, not personal owner liability; counsel to confirm by legal form and province).

**1.3** Keep your organization's profile true and current: legal name, website, and recruiter licences where the law requires them. Tell us within 30 days if your organization changes ownership or control. ⚖️
> *Internal:* RD-002, RD-010 (Ontario ESA licence, Québec CNESST where applicable), TE-02.

**1.4** Membership is not exclusive. You can belong to other networks and work with any recruiter or client.
> *Internal:* TE-04 adopted; TE-05 (ban on competing-network members) rejected.

**1.5** You can leave at any time. Referrals in progress and placements already made continue under their collaboration agreements until they end, including payouts and refunds.
> *Internal:* TE-04, RD-023 (frozen snapshot survives).

**1.6** The Verified badge shows only while your organization is in good standing. It is removed during a network hold or suspension.
> *Internal:* TE-06, RD-002. `[ships with N5]`.

### 2. Using what partners share with you

**2.1** What a partner shares with you (a shared profile, documents, job details, client details) is for one purpose: that referral, for that job and that client.
> *Internal:* TE-09, TE-10. Purpose limitation is also a legal duty (Law 25, PIPEDA). ⚖️

**2.2** Don't use it for anything else. That means no searching for other candidates, jobs or clients with it, no adding the candidate to your database for other work, and no contacting the candidate about other jobs.
> *Internal:* TE-09, TE-10 ("sourcing off"). RD-037 covers how admins assess "derived from" cases.

**2.3** Keep it inside your organization. Don't pass it to anyone outside it, including other agencies, recruiters outside the network or related companies. The only exception is the hiring recruiter sending it to the client for that job. ⚖️
> *Internal:* TE-11 (adapted: serious breach on the sanctions ladder, immediate hold allowed under 9.4, not automatic expulsion). Related orgs use internal referrals.

**2.4** Don't advertise or repost a partner's job anywhere (job boards, social media, your website, newsletters) unless the hiring recruiter agrees in writing first.
> *Internal:* TE-12. The public API already lists only the org's own jobs.

**2.5** If you receive something by mistake, don't use it. Tell the partner and Workatent, and delete any copies.
> *Internal:* TE-09.

**2.6** Access to a shared profile is time-limited. When access ends, or when the candidate revokes their authorization, stop using what you received and delete copies you made outside Workatent, unless the law requires you to keep them. ⚖️
> *Internal:* PD-053.6, RD-038 (timing). Revocation already cuts access immediately. Access expiry `[ships after N3]`; until then, publish only the revocation half.

### 3. Contact with candidates and partners' clients

**3.1** As the hiring recruiter, contact a partner's candidate only to assess them for that job and to run that hiring process. Tell the candidate you are working with {sourcing recruiter} through Workatent.
> *Internal:* TE-14 (adapted: identify the source instead of copying the partner on every message).

**3.2** Don't ask a partner's candidate for a "clean" resume, leads, other candidates or business contacts, and don't invite them to work with you instead of the partner.
> *Internal:* TE-14.

**3.3** As the sourcing recruiter, never contact the hiring recruiter's client about the job, the candidate, interviews, the offer, the fee or payment. All of it goes through the hiring recruiter, including late payment, which you raise on Workatent. This applies for as long as the referral and the resulting placement last. ⚖️
> *Internal:* TE-15, PD-053.5, RD-039 (transaction channel rule, not a non-solicit). Founder to confirm the post-hire part (see RD-039).

**3.4** While your referral is in progress, don't offer your recruiting services to that client, and don't take a direct mandate from the client for the same job unless the hiring recruiter agrees in writing. If the client contacts you about that job or that candidate, send them to the hiring recruiter and tell the hiring recruiter in the referral messages within 2 business days. ⚖️
> *Internal:* PD-053.5 (during the process only), TE-16 (partial), TE-25, RD-039, RD-043.

**3.5** Rule 3.4 doesn't stop you from serving a client you already worked with in the 12 months before your referral was delivered (signed terms, an invoice or an active job you can show). You still can't use that relationship for this candidate, or use anything you learned from the referral. If you already had this same job directly from the client, tell the hiring recruiter before you submit, so you can agree how to work it. ⚖️
> *Internal:* TE-16 prior-relationship exception, RD-039; same-job exception RD-043, RD-013. No duty to disclose your client list to the partner; evidence only if challenged.

**3.6** After a placement, neither the sourcing nor the hiring recruiter approaches the placed candidate about other jobs until the guarantee period ends. If the candidate contacts you first, you may respond. ⚖️
> *Internal:* TE-21 (adapted), RD-040. Counsel to check against Competition Act s.45(1.1).

### 4. Represent only what is yours

**4.1** Submit only candidates your organization represents, with the candidate's authorization for that job and that hiring recruiter, recorded on Workatent. A resume in your database is not authorization.
> *Internal:* TE-23, TE-24, `security-and-privacy.md` (upload is not permission), RD-029.

**4.2** When you record an authorization yourself, you confirm that you or a member of your organization spoke with the candidate about this job, that they are interested, and that what you recorded is true.
> *Internal:* TE-23, RD-041 (reuses the existing confirmation checkbox, reworded).

**4.3** Don't submit another recruiter's candidate, or a candidate you got from someone outside your organization. If another recruiter wants to take part, they can join Workatent.
> *Internal:* TE-24 (no brokering; Workatent does the matching).

**4.4** Open to partners only jobs your organization holds: a real opening, a fee agreement with the end client, and terms that match it. Your ownership window can't be shorter than your client terms.
> *Internal:* TE-23, RD-022 (attestation), RD-028. ⚖️ wording already listed under RD-028.

**4.5** Record the real end client on jobs open to referrals. Only platform admins see it, and only when they need it to resolve a dispute.
> *Internal:* RD-020 (expectation now, required before N3). A fake name ("Client A") that hides the client in a dispute is a breach of 4.8.

**4.6** Keep your jobs current. Close or pause a job when it is filled, on hold or lost. Jobs open to partners must be confirmed every 30 days. If not confirmed within 7 more days, they are paused for partners.
> *Internal:* TE-23, RD-042. `[ships with P1-5]`; until then publish only the first two sentences.

**4.7** Keep your candidates current. Mark a candidate unavailable when they are placed or stop looking.
> *Internal:* TE-23, X5.

**4.8** Everything you state on Workatent must be true: claims, evidence, hire reports, attestations, authorizations and candidate confirmations. Forged evidence and confirmations you control are among the most serious breaches.
> *Internal:* RD-016, RD-017, RD-029, RD-030 (all already call this an RD-002 breach). Triggers 9.4.

### 5. Fair treatment

**5.1** Don't discriminate against candidates on any ground protected by the human rights laws that apply to you, the job or the candidate. This covers job requirements, decisions and feedback. ⚖️
> *Internal:* TE-07 (adapted). Laws by jurisdiction listed for counsel: Canadian Human Rights Act, Ontario Human Rights Code, Québec Charter of human rights and freedoms; US and Brazil when L4 arrives. Matching already excludes protected attributes.

**5.2** Job requirements and decline reasons must be about the job. Don't ask partners to filter candidates by age, sex, origin, family status or any similar ground.
> *Internal:* TE-07, voice-and-tone §2.5.

**5.3** You are responsible for the jobs and candidate information you post. Workatent doesn't verify them. ⚖️
> *Internal:* TE-08, TE-13 (adapted: member accuracy yes; no waiver of Workatent's own security duties).

### 6. Terms, ownership and payouts

**6.1** The terms on a job's card when you submit are the terms for that referral. Later changes apply only to new referrals. Changing an existing referral needs the sourcing recruiter's written agreement before the hire, and no one may make that agreement a condition for moving the candidate forward. ⚖️
> *Internal:* RD-023, RD-024, RD-035.

**6.2** The commission split is fixed (currently 45/45/10) and isn't negotiated per referral.
> *Internal:* RD-005, versioned in `commission_rules`.

**6.3** The first referral delivered wins. If the client hires a referred candidate in any role within the ownership window, the split is owed, even if the referral was declined, withdrawn, cancelled or left unanswered. It isn't owed if the sourcing recruiter released the candidate, or a prior-presentation claim was upheld or not contested.
> *Internal:* RD-003, RD-004, RD-007, RD-015, RD-017.

**6.4** As the hiring recruiter, record every hire of a referred candidate by the client within the window, and record invoices and payments on Workatent. As the sourcing recruiter, if you learn your candidate was hired within the window, report it on Workatent.
> *Internal:* RD-003, RD-030, RD-009 (hire reporting by both sides).

**6.5** The sourcing recruiter's share is computed on the referral's terms. Retainers and engagement fees are part of the fee. If you invoice the client less, the share stays on the terms unless the sourcing recruiter agreed in writing before the change.
> *Internal:* RD-025, RD-026, RD-031, PD-049.

**6.6** As the hiring recruiter, pay the sourcing recruiter's share within 5 business days after the client's payment reaches you. Don't hold it for the guarantee. Late payments show in your record.
> *Internal:* RD-027, RD-018 (calendar), RD-007 ("% paid on time").

**6.7** If a placed candidate leaves during the guarantee, the remedy on the card applies. Refunds are shared pro rata. If there is a replacement, the sourcing recruiter has 10 business days to propose one first.
> *Internal:* RD-006, RD-027. Flow is X6.

**6.8** Workatent's fee and how it is invoiced are set out in `[pricing and fee terms]`.
> *Internal:* RD-008, RD-009, RD-012: `Needs user decision`. Placeholder until decided.

### 7. Disputes

**7.1** Raise problems on Workatent: first in the referral messages, then through a claim, hire report, placement dispute or conduct report. Don't accuse a partner in public and don't retaliate.
> *Internal:* TE-28a (adapted: breach on the ladder, no automatic 2-month suspension).

**7.2** Deadlines matter. A prior-presentation claim must be made within 5 business days of delivery, with dated evidence. A claim can be contested within 10 business days, and a hire report must be answered within 10 business days. If you don't respond in time, we decide on the record. You may involve a lawyer, but that doesn't stop the clock.
> *Internal:* RD-004, RD-017, RD-030, RD-018, TE-28f.

**7.3** Platform admins decide only what happens on Workatent: whether a referral counts, statuses, access, sanctions and the record. They may ask both sides for documents and may contact the candidate. They contact a client only with the hiring recruiter's permission. ⚖️
> *Internal:* PD-053.2, RD-010, TE-28b/c, RD-029 (candidate contact). Admin access to personal information within PD-024 audited exceptions.

**7.4** When no specific rule covers a case, platform admins ask whose action led to the result, based on Workatent records and dated evidence. This never overrides first position, the ownership window, a claim outcome or a release.
> *Internal:* TE-18, RD-037 (residual "but for").

**7.5** Workatent doesn't decide or collect money between members. For a money dispute, the two organizations first negotiate in good faith for 30 days, and Workatent's records are available as evidence. Next comes mediation, then binding arbitration under `[institution]`, seated in `[city]`, under the laws of `[province]`, in `[language]`, with costs allocated `[rule]`. ⚖️
> *Internal:* PD-053.2, TE-28e. 30-day negotiation is a strategist proposal; everything in brackets is for counsel to propose and the founder to approve. Québec: French-first adhesion contract; Brazil/US clauses later.

### 8. Conduct reports

**8.1** If you think a member broke these rules, report it from the referral or the job ("Report a problem"). Say what happened and when, and attach evidence if you have it.
> *Internal:* P0-3 (new `conduct` dispute type). Until it ships: the support address (PD-025).

**8.2** For issues that aren't urgent, you can first try to resolve them with the partner in the referral messages. You can ask us to step in at any time.
> *Internal:* TE-28b (direct conversation first, optional, never required, so the reporter isn't exposed to retaliation).

**8.3** We tell the reported organization what is alleged and give it 10 business days to respond. In the cases in rule 9.4, we act first and notify right after.
> *Internal:* PD-053.3, RD-017 (same 10-business-day rhythm). ⚖️ procedural fairness (CCQ 1375).

**8.4** We share what the other side needs to respond, and we protect candidate and client information while doing it. We aim to respond to a report within 2 business days, and we tell both sides the outcome.
> *Internal:* P0-3 metric (first response ≤ 2 business days). RD-016 (files to admins, not the opponent).

**8.5** Reports made in bad faith are a breach.
> *Internal:* abuse case (reports used as a competitive weapon).

### 9. Sanctions

**9.1** Sanctions have four levels:
1. **Warning:** recorded on your organization's file.
2. **Network hold:** you can't open jobs to partners, submit referrals or accept new ones. Referrals in progress continue. Partners see only that you aren't taking new referrals.
3. **Suspension:** your organization's access to Workatent is suspended.
4. **Termination:** your membership ends.
> *Internal:* PD-053.3, TE-29, TE-30 (adapted). Product gap for PM: today suspension blocks all member access (`organizations.suspended_at`), so a suspended org can't complete in-flight obligations on Workatent. Hold (level 2) is P0-3.

**9.2** We choose the level based on how serious the breach was, the harm done, intent, repeat breaches and cooperation. A serious breach can start at a higher level. Warnings count for 12 months.

**9.3** Before any sanction, we tell you in writing which rule, what we found and the evidence summary, and we give you 10 business days to respond.
> *Internal:* PD-053.3. ⚖️

**9.4** Only two situations allow an immediate network hold without prior notice: data shared outside your organization (rule 2.3), and forged evidence or false candidate confirmations (rule 4.8). We notify you within 1 business day, and the 10-day response window starts then. ⚖️
> *Internal:* PD-053.3 (closed list; nothing else qualifies).

**9.5** During a hold or suspension, your obligations continue. That includes paying partners' shares, refunds and answering disputes. After termination, these obligations still apply until each referral and placement is settled.
> *Internal:* RD-009 analogy (pause new activity, keep in-flight), TE-04.

**9.6** You can ask for one review of a sanction within 10 business days. A different platform admin reviews it.
> *Internal:* strategist decision (cheap due process; the launch checklist already requires two admins). Paid peer panel (TE-28d) deferred to P2-2.

**9.7** An organization whose membership was terminated can reapply after 12 months.
> *Internal:* strategist proposal; reversible.

### 10. Changes to these rules

**10.1** The rules are versioned. The current version and a summary of changes are always available on Workatent.
> *Internal:* TE-03 (adapted), P0-2.

**10.2** For material changes, we give 30 days' notice, and your organization admin and every member must accept the new version.
> *Internal:* PD-053.4.

**10.3** If your organization hasn't accepted by the effective date, it can't start new partner activity until it does. Referrals in progress continue. You can leave instead (rule 1.5).
> *Internal:* strategist decision, same effect as a network hold but with no breach recorded.

**10.4** A change to the rules never changes the terms of referrals already submitted.
> *Internal:* RD-023, RD-024.

**10.5** If a law requires a change sooner, we will tell you and apply it from the date the law requires. ⚖️

**10.6** The rules are available in French, English and Portuguese. In Québec, the French version is offered first. ⚖️
> *Internal:* PD-053.7, Charter of the French language (Bill 96). Which version governs is for counsel: `[placeholder]`.

## Not in v1, on purpose

| Not in v1 | Why |
|---|---|
| Non-circumvention fee on off-platform splits | Founder rejected (PD-053.1). Retention must come from product value. The RD-008 "pairs that stop after one placement" signal is tracked only. |
| Client non-solicitation after the process (post-hire or post-closure) | PD-053.5: waits for the competition-law opinion (s.45(1)(b), ancillary-restraint defence). Recommended post-opinion scope in RD-039. |
| Split owed on re-placement of a placed candidate with a different client | Would be candidate-global ownership, rejected in RD-003. The client's own off-limits terms and rule 3.6 cover the real risk (RD-040). |
| Candidate ownership with no time limit | Contradicts RD-003. The "old resume" risk is handled by rules 2.1/2.6 and access expiry (RD-038). |
| Workatent as binding arbiter of money | PD-053.2, RD-010. |
| Automatic fixed-length suspensions, expulsion without a hearing, holds "for any reason" | Not proportionate, Québec adhesion-contract risk ⚖️ (TE-11, TE-28a, TE-30). Replaced by the ladder and the closed 9.4 list. |
| Amending the rules "on publication" | Replaced by 30-day notice and re-acceptance (PD-053.4). |
| Ban on members of competing networks | Hurts liquidity, competition risk (TE-05). |
| Paid peer-review panel | The network is too small for anonymity. Free contest is part of our promise (RD-016/017). Revisit at about 150 active orgs (P2-2). |
| "Share your next two commissions" as compensation | Off-platform and impossible to check (TE-16). |
| Contract / temp splits | Not doing (PD-010, RD-022). |
| Full liability waiver, including security failures | Can't waive Law 25 / PIPEDA duties (TE-13) ⚖️. Terms will have a reasonable limitation instead. |
| Duty to tell partners who your clients are ("niche locking") | Anti-competitive signal. We don't ask for client lists (TE-16). |
| Rules for using the Verified badge outside Workatent | P2-3, after N5. |
| Blocking another organization | A product feature (P1-1), not a member obligation. Can be mentioned in help content when it ships. |
